Decide what to eat in 10 seconds

MealRoll ends decision fatigue. Roll for a meal that fits your mood and the time you have, cook it with what's already in your kitchen, or find a restaurant nearby.

What the app does

  • Roll for meals — a recipe picked for your vibe, time and taste.
  • Cook with what you have — scan your fridge, get matching recipes.
  • Find restaurants — search nearby places by dish, cuisine or ingredient.
  • Smart Pantry — track what you own and use it up before it expires.
  • Track your habits — weekly report, food spending and nutrition insights.
