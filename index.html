✦ Frequently asked

Everything you need to know

MealRoll is designed to make food choices faster without taking control away from you.

Get MealRoll
How does the meal roll work?

MealRoll combines your time, mood, preferences, ingredients and context to suggest a meal that fits the moment.

Can I use MealRoll without scanning food?

Yes. Scanning is optional. You can roll immediately, add ingredients manually, browse recipes or search restaurants.

Does Smart Pantry help reduce waste?

Smart Pantry helps you remember what you have, highlight items that need using and plan meals around them first.

Is MealRoll available on iPhone and Android?

Yes — MealRoll runs on both iPhone and Android. Use the download buttons above to get it from the App Store or Google Play.
